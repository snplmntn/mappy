About this map

The map of SM Makati that Mappy uses to find stores, plan your stops and walk you there, floor by floor.

A hackathon submission

Mappy is our entry to the AppBuildersPH Hackathon 2026, under the theme Local AI. It's a working prototype built during the event, not an official SM app, and the map is a demo map made for it.

What's real, and what isn't

  • The building outline is real. It comes from OpenStreetMap.
  • Store names are real, taken from public store listings. Their floors were checked against a June 2026 walk-through video of the mall. The video skips the Annex, the Lower Ground corridors and Cyberzone, so stores there are unconfirmed.
  • The inside layout is our reconstruction. No public indoor map of the mall exists, so where each store sits is our best estimate. Use the mall's own directory for exact locations.
  • A few stalls are made up for the demo: FixHub Mobile, QuickFix Gadget Clinic and ScreenDoc, the phone-repair stalls marked "demo".

How directions work

Mappy finds the shortest walk along the mall's corridors and estimates the time from the distance. To change floors it uses the escalators, which only run one way, or the elevator.

Pushing a stroller or using a wheelchair? Turn on Elevators only in your plan and every route avoids escalators.

Works offline, stays private

The map and the AI that reads your messages both run on a laptop here in the mall. Your phone talks only to that laptop over local Wi-Fi, so Mappy works with no internet at all, and what you type never leaves the building.
